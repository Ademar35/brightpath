## Strengths (keep these)
- Every unit has a clear Grammar / Vocabulary / Pronunciation split. That maps straight onto the app's lesson structure.
- It's very local: candongueiro, Roque Santeiro, mucua, Benfica, Huambo, Benguela, kwanzas. It also uses the European/Angolan standard (telemóvel, autocarro, sumo, pequeno-almoço, "estou a + verb", dezasseis), which is correct for Angola.
- The pronunciation targets (ão, nh, qu) are the sounds foreigners struggle with most.
- The topics are useful for survival: shopping, directions, the doctor.
- It already has a cast: Isa, Márcio, Paulo, Sofia, Carlos, Carla, Pedro, Ana, Marta. **Isa becomes the app's host character.**

## Gaps to fill
1. **Units 10, 11, 12 and the verb appendix are missing.** The contents page has no titles for them, and Unit 5 points to verb tables on pp. 113-116 that I can't find.
2. **Numbers 21 to 999 are never taught.** Unit 2 stops at 20, and Unit 7 jumps straight to millions of kwanzas.
3. **No telling the time**, even though exercises use "às 6h" and "às 13h".
4. **Units 1 and 2 use estar** ("estou no mercado") before it's taught in Unit 3.
5. **Unit 4's goal is "Dizer o que comeu"** (past tense), and it uses "provou", but only the present tense is taught. "Degustam" also appears without explanation.
6. **Unit 6 is thin.** Possessives and plurals are named in the header but never explained.
7. **Unit 9 repeats itself.** Sections 1 to 6 appear twice, and there are no exercises, although the contents page lists "Exercício prático".
8. **Unit 7 repeats** the same price table and questions on p.53 and p.60.
9. **No answer key.** The app needs one; I'll draft it and the teacher checks it.
10. **Missing Angolan culture:** foods (funje, muamba, calulu, mufete, kizaca, gindungo, kissângua), transport (candongueiro, kupapata), respect and register (mais velho, kota, o senhor / a senhora), and common slang (bué, mambo).
11. **The "Escute e repita" exercises have no audio.** The app solves this.

## How each unit becomes a week in the app
Almost every booklet exercise is one of 7 activity types: **gap-fill, word order, matching, multiple choice, true/false, listen and repeat, free writing**. The app builds these 7 once, and every unit reuses them, so most of the book converts directly. On top of that, each unit gets one signature game and one mission:

| Week | Signature game | Mission |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hora Certa:** swipe the right greeting for the time of day | Greet people at the market, morning to night |
| 2 | **Liga-me:** hear a 9xx phone number and dial it | Swap numbers with Márcio |
| 3 | **Ser ou Estar:** a fast sorting game | Get through passport control at Luanda airport |
| 4 | **Mesa Angolana:** build an order | Order lunch at a restaurant on the Ilha |
| 5 | **Um Dia em Luanda:** put a routine in time order | Tell Isa about your day |
| 6 | **Árvore:** build a family tree | Introduce your family on a video call |
| 7 | **Mercado:** bargain in kwanzas | Buy clothes at a market stall |
| 8 | **Candongueiro Rush:** follow directions on a map | Find the pharmacy when lost |
| 9 | **Triagem:** match symptoms to the right specialist | Call for help in an emergency (role-play) |
| 10-12 | Added when the teacher finishes these units | |

**This changes the season from 8 weeks to 12 weeks, one unit per week.** Weeks 1 to 9 can launch now, and 10 to 12 are added later. The missing pieces (numbers 21-999, time, Angolan food and culture) become short "bonus" mini-lessons placed where they're needed, so the teacher doesn't need to rewrite the book.

## Answer key: Unit 1 (draft for the teacher to check)

**Complete with ser (p.3):** 1 sou, 2 é, 3 somos, 4 és, 5 é, 6 são, 7 é, 8 são, 9 é, 10 és.

**Unit 1 exercise, grammar 1 (p.7):** 1 sou, 2 é, 3 somos, 4 é, 5 é, 6 és, 7 sou, 8 é, 9 somos, 10 são.

**Pronouns (p.7):** 1 Eu, 2 Tu, 3 Ele / Ela / Você, 4 Nós, 5 Eles / Elas / Vocês, 6 Eu, 7 Tu, 8 Ela, 9 Nós, 10 Eles / Elas / Vocês.
(This exercise uses estar, which isn't taught until Unit 3. The app uses ser sentences instead.)

**A or O (p.8):** 1 O, 2 O / A (motorista works for both), 3 O, 4 O / A, 5 A, 6 O, 7 O / A, 8 A, 9 O / A, 10 O / A.
Several of these nouns (motorista, cliente, estudante, segurança, colega) are the same for men and women. That's worth a short note in the booklet, because learners will think the exercise has errors.

**Numbers in words (p.8):** três, sete, dez, dois, cinco, um, oito, quatro, seis, nove.

**Greetings (p.9):** 1 Bom dia, 2 Boa noite, 3 Tchau / Até amanhã, 4 Boa tarde, 5 Bom dia. Underline: Bom dia, Boa noite, Tchau, Olá, 12h.

**Sound -ão (p.10):** camião, coração, pão, irmão, feijão, estação, limão, mão.

**Word order (p.10):** 1 Há pão no mercado. 2 Ele é o meu irmão. 3 Estou na estação de táxi. 4 Eles são angolanos. 5 Eles não estão aqui.
